# Grab Bag
Grab Bag is a Balatro mod that currently adds: 
- 133 Jokers
  - 28 Common Jokers
  - 37 Unommon Jokers
  - 10 Rare Jokers
  - 38 Boss Jokers
  - 25 Shattered Jokers
- 2 Consumable Sets
  - 20 Ephemeral Cards
  - 13 Hex Cards
- 8 Booster Packs 
  - 4 Ephemeral Packs
  - 4 Hex Packs
- New Card Modifier
  - 12 Hexes
- 6 Hex Enhancements
- 5 Spectrals
- 3 Seals
- 12 Decks
- 30 Boss Blinds
- 5 Showdown Boss Blinds
- 1 Edition
- and 7 Challenges.

The content in this mod is supposed to feel vanilla, and I created this new content to be largely in line with the rules and design philosophy that localthunk follows when making Jokers and other content.
